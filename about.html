I’m Nikolas Roumeliotis, a filmmaker, editor and digital artist based in Athens.

Editing is at the centre of my practice. I work with filmmakers and other artists to shape footage into a story — finding its rhythm, its tension and the moments that stay with you.

My work also moves through directing, VFX, stop-motion and interactive media. With a background in computer engineering and studies at Stavrakos Film School and Prague Film School, I bring both a cinematic and a technical approach to collaboration.

I’m interested in working with people who have something to say, and finding the right form to say it together.
