I’m Nikolas Roumeliotis, a filmmaker, editor and digital artist based in Athens.

Editing is the core of my work. I’m interested in how a pause, the length of a shot or a change in sequence can shift the way a scene feels. I work closely with filmmakers and artists to find the right form for each story.

Alongside editing, I direct films and explore stop-motion and interactive media. My background in computer engineering and studies at Stavrakos Film School and Prague Film School inform both the creative and technical sides of my practice.

If you’re developing a film, a music video or an experimental project, I’d like to hear about it.
